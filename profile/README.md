### ＃ Production works
Not demos — production systems built in small teams, usually in pairs, often two jobs at once. I've shipped national-scale platforms, internal tools, and fullstack products across six major projects: a multilingual therapy platform for kids, teens, adults, and couples; three separate Vue applications for the National Iranian Gas Company (CRM, Field PWA, and Gaziom), each with its own visual identity; a reservation-first cafe system built around consultants and brokers; a professional qualification platform that maps career gaps and tells users exactly what to learn next; a full-lifecycle project and task management system; and a business diagnostics platform that scores organizations against EFQM, BPMM, APQC, and Palladium maturity models. Alongside these I've delivered a long tail of smaller builds — cafe PWAs, dental clinic PWAs, online shops, and assorted internal tools — but the six below are the ones that ran at real scale. Some public, some behind closed doors. All of them built to run, and all of them built with other people.
